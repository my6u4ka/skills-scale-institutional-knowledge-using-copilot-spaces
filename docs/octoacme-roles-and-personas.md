# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategy. They validate that features meet acceptance criteria, identify defects, and ensure release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with project scope
- Define acceptance criteria with Product and Development leads
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Identify and triage defects
- Validate release readiness before deployment
- Collaborate on test infrastructure and CI/CD integration

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality standards before release
- Reduce production defects and post-release issues
- Provide clear visibility into test coverage and risk

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Partner on test automation, define testability requirements, and review implementation for quality
- **With Product Managers**: Align on acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- **With Project Managers**: Report quality status, track defect resolution, and validate release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and daily standups
- Test plan reviews and acceptance criteria alignment
- Defect reports and QA status updates
- Pre-release quality gate reviews

---

## Design/UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads define user experience, visual design standards, and usability requirements. They ensure products are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with brand standards.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define UX requirements
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specs
- Define accessibility and usability standards
- Review designs and implementation for UX compliance
- Collaborate with Product and Development on trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce post-launch UX issues and user confusion
- Maintain design consistency and brand alignment

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Translate product requirements into UX specs and user flows
- **With Developers**: Provide design specifications and conduct design reviews during implementation
- **With Project Managers**: Advise on timeline impacts for design work and usability testing

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff and planning (UX requirements and design timeline)
- Design reviews and code reviews
- Usability testing and feedback sessions
- Design system and accessibility standard documentation

---

## DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers design, build, and maintain deployment infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and production monitoring systems. They enable safe, reliable releases and operational visibility.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure and deployment automation
- Set up monitoring, logging, and alerting
- Define deployment and rollback procedures
- Collaborate on performance and security requirements
- Support incident response and post-mortems

### Goals
- Enable safe, reliable, and frequent deployments
- Minimize deployment risk and downtime
- Provide operational visibility and incident response capability

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Support deployment practices, provide infrastructure as code templates, and troubleshoot production issues
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate deployment windows and communicate release readiness
- **With QA/Testing**: Ensure staging environment matches production for accurate testing

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Infrastructure design reviews
- Incident response and post-incident reviews
- Monitoring dashboards and performance metrics

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural direction, define technical standards, and mitigate technical risks. They guide design decisions and ensure code quality and system reliability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design standards
- Review technical designs and high-risk implementations
- Identify and mitigate technical debt and risks
- Mentor developers and support capability building
- Collaborate with Product on technical feasibility and trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable, and reliable systems
- Reduce technical risk and long-term maintenance burden
- Support continuous improvement in code quality and practices

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide technical guidance, conduct design reviews, and mentor on best practices
- **With Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility, trade-offs, and long-term architectural implications
- **With Project Managers**: Assess technical risks and help communicate technical complexity to stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Code review and mentoring
- Risk assessment and mitigation planning
- Technical documentation and standards

---

## Sponsor/Business Owner

### Role Summary
Sponsors are business stakeholders who approve project scope, budget, and major decisions. They provide business context and escalation authority.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals and success metrics
- Approve scope, timeline, and resource allocation
- Serve as escalation point for major decisions
- Communicate project value to broader organization
- Monitor business outcomes and ROI

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Provide governance and risk oversight

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Review progress, approve scope changes, and escalate blockers
- **With Product Managers**: Align on business priorities, success metrics, and strategic fit
- **With Delivery Team**: Provide business context and strategic direction

### Typical Communication
- Project approval and initiation
- Monthly status and milestone reviews
- Escalation and decision-making
- Budget and resource approval

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach (Optional)

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile practices, remove process impediments, and support continuous team improvement. They coach the team on agile methodologies and help maintain team health.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, retrospectives, reviews)
- Remove impediments and blockers to team progress
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track velocity and team metrics
- Facilitate retrospectives and action item follow-up

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and productivity
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Maintain psychological safety and team cohesion

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate on scheduling, escalation of impediments, and metrics reporting
- **With Developers**: Remove blockers, facilitate collaboration, and support team development
- **With All Roles**: Model agile values and facilitate team alignment

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Impediment tracking and resolution
- Retrospectives and action item tracking
- Team health and velocity metrics

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When defining cross-functional projects, ensure clear ownership and interaction patterns between roles to minimize gaps and duplication.
